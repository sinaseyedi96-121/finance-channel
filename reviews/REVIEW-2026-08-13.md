> `"support": 29.22, "resistance": 33.25, "last_price": 31.48`

At $31.48, the distance to resistance $33.25 is **5.6%**, while distance to support $29.22 is **7.2%** — a roughly 0.78 R:R as stated.

The problem: the caption calls this "unfavorable until 200-day reclaimed," yet the **key level it names, $32.81, is inside the drawn support/resistance band and is not in the chart object at all.** A chart-literate reader sees the real decision level as $33.25 (the drawn resistance) — price is 5.6% below it, not sitting at a neutral pivot. The post is structurally coherent only if you accept an invisible EMA200 level overriding the visible S/R lines, which is exactly the "caption and chart imply different things" failure mode.
